Ground shipping that makes customers smile.

Amazon Shipping is a package delivery service powered by Amazon's transportation network, offering scalable capacity, proactive support, and innovative solutions for brands of all sizes.

Innovation that moves the industry forward

Amazon Shipping is a network built for what's next. Your business connects seamlessly to the Amazon logistics network, ensuring your brand delivers great customer experiences at every step.

Your eyes on the ground

From weather events to port congestion, things happen in logistics. And while no shipper can make everything go right, we can help minimize disruptions. Proactive communication and alerts help you take action before problems arise, and stay ahead in every season.

Sustainability from order to door

In 2019 Amazon co-founded the Climate Pledge, a commitment to be net-zero carbon by 2040. Amazon is committed to de-carbonizing its delivery fleet company-wide and has rolled out more than 20,000 custom electric delivery vans across the U.S.
