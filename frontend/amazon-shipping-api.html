Frequently asked questions

Shipping Requirements

Yes, Amazon Shipping can deliver orders placed on your own website, Amazon.com, and other selling channels. Our service integrates with many popular e-commerce platforms and shipping software.

Amazon Shipping accepts certain dangerous goods that comply with DOT regulations. Please review our Dangerous Goods policy or contact our support team for specific requirements and restrictions.

Amazon Shipping accepts packages up to 50 lbs and with dimensions up to 108 inches in length or 165 inches in length plus girth. Special rates may apply for oversized packages.

Amazon Shipping offers ground shipping service across the 48 contiguous US states, with reliable transit times to approximately 95% of the US population. Pickup areas are expanding daily.

Purchasing Labels

You can purchase labels through our web portal, Amazon Shipping API, or integrated third-party software partners like ShipStation, EasyPost, and others.

Shipping labels are typically valid for 30 days from the date of creation. After this period, you will need to void the label and create a new one.

Tracking

You can track shipments using our online tracking tool at track.amazon.com, through the Amazon Shipping portal, or via API. Simply enter your tracking ID to see real-time updates.

Yes, recipients can track their packages using the tracking ID at track.amazon.com. They'll see the same real-time updates available to shippers.
